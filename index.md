Hello, I am Noara Razzak, a PhD Candidate in the John E. Walker Department of Economics at Clemson University. I conduct research on financial markets and community banking with aligned interests in industrial organization, urban economics and applied econometrics.  My research interests include studying credit accessibility of racially diverse communities along with the institutional and geographical features of the credit markets that these communities can access. My [current working paper](https://noararazzak.com/jmp/jmp_abstract_razzak_2025.pdf) studies the effect of Minority Depository Institutions' branch closures in their local markets.
