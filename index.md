Hello, I am a PhD Candidate in the John E. Walker Department of Economics at Clemson University. I research financial institutions, community banking, and household finance, connecting these areas to urban economics and applied econometrics. Specifically my research focuses on studying credit accessibility of diverse urban communities. My [current working paper](https://noararazzak.com/jmp/jmp_razzak_noara.pdf) studies the impact of closing urban community bank branches, Minority Depository Institutions (MDIs) in their local markets. I am on the 2025-2026 Job Market. You can find my CV [here](https://noararazzak.com/cv/cv_razzak_noara.pdf) and contact me at: <nrazzak@g.clemson.edu>. 

I will present my research at the Southern Economic Association's 95th Annual Meeting in Tampa and the Southern Finance Association's 65th Annual Meeting in Orlando - both scheduled in November.
