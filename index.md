Hello, I am a PhD Candidate in the John E. Walker Department of Economics at Clemson University. I conduct research on financial insitutions, community banking and household finance with aligned interests in urban economics and applied econometrics. My research interests focus on studying credit accessibility of diverse urban communities. My [current working paper](https://noararazzak.com/jmp/jmp_razzak_noara.pdf) studies the effect of closing urban community bank branches, Minority Depository Institutions (MDIs) in their local markets.
