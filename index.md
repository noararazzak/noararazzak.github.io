Hello, I am a PhD Candidate in the John E. Walker Department of Economics at Clemson University. I research financial institutions, community banking, and household finance, connecting these areas to industrial organization and urban economics. Specifically my research focuses on studying credit accessibility of underserved and diverse urban communities in the US and the influence of internet banking, non-bank lenders, and fintech in addressing the credit access gap created by the decline in bank branches nationally.
